2

Create your wallet

Two secrets: a password and a recovery phrase. They do different jobs.

A long, unique password

4 or more random words, or 16+ characters from a manager. Never reuse one.

Recovery phrase on paper

Settings › Security › Recovery phrase

Write it by hand, check every word, and do it somewhere private.

Two copies, two places

Fires and moves happen. A steel plate survives both.

Test your backup

Restore with a tiny amount before you move real money in.

Encrypted backup file

Settings › Backup and export

A handy extra that needs your password. It doesn't replace the paper.

Do

  • Write it on paper, by hand
  • Keep it where only you can reach it
  • Tell someone you trust where it is, not what it is

Never

  • Photograph it or save it in notes, email or cloud
  • Type it into a website, form or support chat
  • Share it with anyone, including "support"
Sillet and Raton will never ask for your phrase. Anyone who does is scamming you.
3

Turn on the protections

Open Settings, then Security and Privacy. Most are on already, check them anyway.

Password to send

Security › Sending

Asks for it on every send, even when the wallet is already unlocked.

Auto-lock

Security › Locking

Set 5 minutes or less. Also lock on sleep and when minimized.

Lock the file to this device

A stolen copy of the wallet file is useless on another computer.

Extra check for large sends

Security › Large sends

Over your limit, you type the last 4 characters of the address.

Clipboard protection

Privacy

Clears copied addresses and keeps them out of clipboard history.

New address warning

The first send to an address you've never used gets flagged.

Slow down wrong passwords

After 3 misses each try waits longer, and you see how many happened.

Windows Hello or fingerprint

Security › Passkey

Quick unlock. Your password is still the real key.

Hide from screenshots

Privacy

Blocks screen capture and blurs Sillet when you switch apps.

Harden the Sillet process

Makes it harder for other programs to read Sillet's memory.

Only send to contacts

The strictest mode. Blocks any address you have not saved.

Payment notifications

An alert you didn't expect is often the first warning.

Run the Security checkup in Settings to see what is on and what is missing.
4

Everyday habits

Settings help. Habits stop most attacks.

Check the address

Compare the first and last characters with the source. Malware swaps copies.

Send a test first

New address or a big amount? Send a little, wait, then send the rest.

Check the network

USDT sent on the wrong network can be lost for good.

Read the review screen

It shows what they get, the fee and your total. The fee goes on top.

Lock when you leave

Auto-lock is the safety net, not the plan.

Keep it updated

Update only from the official page, same as the first install.

Slow down

Urgency is how scams work. Real support never needs your phrase.

Fine

  • Typing addresses of sites you use
  • Saving trusted contacts by name
  • Your own network or mobile data

Red flags

  • Anyone offering to "fix" your wallet
  • Links from DMs, ads or surprise emails
  • Giveaways and guaranteed returns

6

If something goes wrong

Act in this order.

Lost or broken device

Funds are fine. Install Sillet, choose Restore and enter your phrase.

Phrase or wallet exposed

New wallet on a clean device, move everything now. A new password won't help.

Sent to a scammer

It can't be undone. Save the tx id, report it, and ignore anyone offering recovery.

Forgot your password

Restore with your phrase and set a new one. Without both, nobody can help.
