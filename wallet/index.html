FAQ

Questions

Is Sillet really free?

Yes. There is no subscription and no ads. You only pay the normal network fee when you send, and that goes to the network, not to us

Can the Raton team see or move my funds?

No. Your keys are generated and encrypted on your device and never sent anywhere. We have no account, no server and no way to touch them

What happens if I lose my computer?

Install Sillet anywhere and restore with your recovery phrase. The same words bring back the same addresses and funds

What if I forget my password?

Use your recovery phrase to restore the wallet and set a new password. Keep the phrase on paper, somewhere safe and private

Does it work with my hardware wallet?

Yes, as watch-only. Add your Ledger or Trezor addresses to follow balances and get receive addresses. Sending stays on the device app
